Work permits (EAD): the basics

September 27, 2026 · 1 min read · Vista Immigrant Support

An Employment Authorization Document (EAD), often called a work permit, is a card issued by USCIS that shows you are allowed to work in the United States. You apply for it on Form I-765.

Who can apply?

Eligibility depends on your immigration situation. People in several different situations may be able to apply, for example people with a pending asylum application, a pending green card application, or certain temporary protections. A work permit is requested under a specific category, so the right category matters.

Common mistakes

  • Applying under the wrong category
  • Missing supporting documents or photos
  • Waiting until the card is about to expire to renew
  • Moving and not updating your address with USCIS

Plan your renewal

Processing times change and can be long. USCIS generally lets you request a renewal well before the expiration date, so mark the date on your calendar early. Whether you can keep working while a renewal is pending depends on your category and the rules at the time.

Getting help

Our team can help you gather documents, photos and copies. For advice about which category applies to you, our licensed attorney can speak with you about matters she accepts.

Common questions

Before you call

Is Vista a law firm?

No. Vista Immigrant Support Inc. is not a law firm and our non-attorney staff do not give legal advice. Legal advice and representation are provided by licensed attorneys for the matters they accept.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Our office is open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, by appointment only. You can book online or call us.

What language can I speak with you?

Tell us your language when you call or book. We will do our best to arrange an interpreter for your visit.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring any immigration papers you have, a photo ID, and notices from USCIS or the court. If you are unsure, bring everything and we will help you sort through it.

What we offer

Immigration Services

Support from the Vista team, and legal representation from a licensed attorney for matters the attorney accepts.

Legal representation — by our licensed attorney, for matters accepted

  • Asylum Applications

    Protection for people in the United States who fear serious harm if they return home.

    →

  • Work Permits (EAD)

    Applications and renewals for an Employment Authorization Document.

    →

  • Green Card / Adjustment of Status

    Applying for lawful permanent residence while you are in the United States.

    →

  • Family-Based Immigration Matters

    Petitions and applications to keep families together.

    →

  • Removal Proceedings

    Representation in immigration court when the government seeks removal.

    →

  • Master Calendar Hearings

    The short first hearings where the judge sets the course of your case.

    →

  • Individual Hearings

    The full hearing where you present your case to the judge.

    →

  • Motions to Reopen

    Asking the court to reopen a case after a decision has been made.

    →

  • Immigration Appeals

    Appealing an immigration judge’s decision to the Board of Immigration Appeals.

    →

  • USCIS Interviews

    Preparing for and attending your interview with a USCIS officer.

    →

  • Requests for Evidence (RFE)

    Responding to a USCIS notice asking for more documents.

    →

Support services — provided by Vista

  • Immigration Document Assistance

    Help gathering, organizing, copying and scanning your paperwork.

    →

  • Translation Services

    Written translation of documents into English.

    →

  • Interpreter Services

    Spoken interpretation so you can be understood at your appointment.

    →
Legal advice and representation are provided only by licensed attorneys, for the matters they accept. Vista staff who are not attorneys do not provide legal advice.

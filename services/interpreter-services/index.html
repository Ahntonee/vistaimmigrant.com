Interpreter Services

Spoken interpretation so you can be understood at your appointment.

Understanding what is said, and being understood, matters in every meeting about your case. Our interpreters help you communicate during appointments at our office and can help you prepare for other meetings.

An interpreter conveys what is said; they do not give legal advice or opinions about your case. For hearings in immigration court, the court provides an interpreter at no cost, and you should ask for one in advance.

Who this is for

  • People who are more comfortable speaking a language other than English
  • Families who need help at meetings with the attorney or our team

What to expect

Tell us your language when you schedule. We will arrange an interpreter for your appointment whenever we can, and confirm it with you before the day.

What to bring

  • Your appointment details
  • Any documents you want to discuss
  • A list of questions you would like to ask
This is a support service. It is not legal advice. For legal advice or representation, we can connect you with our licensed attorney.

Questions

Will the court give me an interpreter?

Yes. Immigration court provides an interpreter at no cost for hearings. Make sure the court knows which language you speak.

Next step

Call or request a time and we will talk through your situation in private.
